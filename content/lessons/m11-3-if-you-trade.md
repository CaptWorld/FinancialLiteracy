:::hook
Most people who lose heavily in trading didn't plan to. They started with "just ₹10,000 to learn", had a lucky month, raised the stakes, hit a bad week, and then tried to win it back. **Rules written before the first trade are the only defence that works.**
:::

## Guardrails, written before you start

1. **Learning money only**: a fixed amount you could lose entirely without touching goals, emergency fund, EPF or family money. Many people choose a tiny % of their investments.
2. **No borrowed money, ever**: no credit cards, personal loans or margin funding.
3. **Limits**: maximum loss per day and per month. Hit it and you stop for that period.
4. **Position size**: never more than a small slice of the learning money in one trade.
5. **Journal every trade**: date, instrument, why, entry, exit, result after costs, emotion.
6. **Annual verdict**: after 12 months, compare net results (after costs and tax) with simply holding a Nifty index fund. If you're behind, and most people are, stop.

## Taxes on trading

Trading income isn't taxed like long-term investing:

- **Intraday share trading**: treated as **speculative business income**.
- **F&O**: treated as **non-speculative business income**.
- You may need **ITR-3**, books of accounts, and in some cases a **tax audit**, depending on turnover and profit. **Report losses properly** so you can carry them forward.

These rules have conditions and thresholds that change, and moved into the Income-tax Act, 2025 from April 2026. A CA is worth it if you trade actively (Module 12).

## Scams around trading

:::trap
- **Fake trading apps** promoted in WhatsApp/Telegram "investment groups": they show fake profits, then block withdrawals or ask for "tax" or "fees" to release money.
- **"Guaranteed" algo bots and copy-trading** with monthly subscriptions.
- **Account-handling**: giving someone your broker login to "trade for you". Illegal, and your account takes the losses.
- **Loss-recovery agents** who contact victims promising to recover money for an upfront fee: a second scam.

Use only SEBI-registered brokers' official apps. Report fraud on **1930** or cybercrime.gov.in.
:::

:::checklive cybercrime
Report online financial fraud quickly; speed improves the chance of freezing the money.
:::

## When trading becomes a problem

Warning signs: chasing losses with bigger bets, borrowing to trade, hiding trades from family, checking positions compulsively, trading affecting sleep, work or relationships.

If several apply, **stop trading and tell someone you trust**. Close the F&O segment on your account (brokers let you deactivate it). Problem trading behaves like problem gambling, and help from a counsellor or doctor works.
