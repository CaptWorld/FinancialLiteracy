:::hook
Many salaried people never file a return because "the company already deducts tax". Then a notice arrives about unreported FD interest, or a refund they were owed sits unclaimed, or a loss they could have carried forward is lost. **Filing yourself is a 30–60 minute job once you know the steps.**
:::

## Step 1: gather documents

| Document | Where | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 130** (formerly Form 16) | HR, after year-end | Salary, deductions claimed, TDS by employer |
| **AIS / TIS** | Income tax portal | Everything reported about you: salary, interest, dividends, share/MF trades, high-value transactions |
| **Form 168** (formerly 26AS) | Income tax portal | Tax credits: TDS, TCS, advance tax paid |
| Bank interest certificates | Bank apps | Savings and FD interest |
| Capital-gains statement | Mutual fund CAS, broker | Gains and losses, short/long-term |
| Proofs (old regime only) | You | Rent receipts, section 123 investments, health insurance premiums, home-loan certificate |

## Step 2: cross-check AIS

Compare AIS with your own records. Missing income → add it to your return. **Wrong entries** (e.g. a transaction that isn't yours, or counted twice) → give feedback through the AIS feedback option before filing.

:::checklive it-ais-faq
The department's AIS guide: what it contains and how to give feedback on wrong entries.
:::

## Step 3: choose the form

- **ITR-1 (Sahaj)**: simple cases. Salary, one house property, interest, and limited long-term equity gains, within the form's income limits.
- **ITR-2**: capital gains beyond ITR-1's limits, more than one house property, foreign assets or income.
- **ITR-3**: business or professional income, **including F&O and intraday trading**.

The portal helps you pick, and form rules change slightly most years.

## Step 4: file and verify

1. Log in at incometax.gov.in with your PAN.
2. Start the return for the right tax year; most data is **pre-filled** from Form 130 and AIS.
3. Check every section, add missing income, choose your **regime**.
4. See the result: **refund** or **tax payable**. Pay any balance online before submitting.
5. Submit, then **e-verify** (Aadhaar OTP is quickest) within the allowed time. **Unverified = not filed.**
6. Track the refund on the portal. It goes to your **pre-validated bank account**.

## Deadlines and mistakes

- Due date for most salaried individuals is usually **31 July** after the tax year ends. Check each year.
- Late filing: a fee, interest on unpaid tax, and you **can't carry forward most capital losses**.
- Made a mistake? You can file a **revised** return within the allowed period, and later an **updated** return in some cases (with extra tax).

:::trap
Tax-refund SMS and "income tax officer" calls are a top fraud. **Refunds are never released by clicking a link or sharing an OTP.** Notices are visible on the e-filing portal under your login. Always check there.
:::

:::dive When to pay for help
A CA is worth it if you have F&O/intraday trading, business or freelance income, foreign assets or RSUs from a foreign employer, property sales, or a notice you don't understand. For simple salary + interest + mutual fund gains, filing yourself is very doable, and doing it once teaches you a lot about your own money.
:::
