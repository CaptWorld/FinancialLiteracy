:::hook
Your offer letter says ₹12 LPA. Divide by 12 and you expect ₹1 lakh a month. The first salary credit is much less. **Nobody stole it. The CTC was never your salary.**
:::

## The three numbers

1. **CTC (Cost to Company)**: the yearly total the employer spends on you.
2. **Gross salary**: what you earn each month before deductions.
3. **Net / in-hand**: what lands in your bank account.

## What sits inside CTC

| Part | Comes monthly? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Yes | Base for PF, gratuity, HRA calculations |
| HRA | Yes | Can reduce tax under the old regime if you pay rent |
| Special / other allowances | Yes | Fully taxable usually |
| Employer PF | No, goes to your EPF account | Yours, but locked for the long term |
| Gratuity provision | No | Paid only when you leave after qualifying service |
| Insurance premiums | No | Group health/term cover, valuable but not cash |
| Variable pay / bonus | Later, maybe | Depends on rating/company results |
| Joining bonus, ESOPs/RSUs | One-time / vesting | Often conditional |

## From gross to in-hand

**In-hand = Gross − Employee PF − Professional tax − TDS − other deductions** (meal card, loan recovery, voluntary PF, etc.)

:::example
Illustration only, with made-up round numbers.
Monthly gross ₹80,000. Employee PF ₹3,000. Professional tax ₹200. TDS ₹6,000.
In-hand = 80,000 − 3,000 − 200 − 6,000 = **₹70,800**.
Your real payslip will differ: PF depends on your basic and the PF rules, TDS on your tax regime and declarations.
:::

## The Labour Codes changed salary structures

Four Labour Codes came into force on **21 November 2025**. For PF and gratuity, "wages" (basic + DA + retaining allowance) must now be **at least 50% of total remuneration**; allowances above that are added back. Many companies restructured salaries with a bigger basic. That can mean **slightly lower in-hand but higher PF and gratuity**, i.e. more forced long-term saving.

:::checklive labour-faq
Read questions 2–4 on the "wages" definition and the 50% rule. Then compare with your payslip: is basic (+DA) at least half of your total pay?
:::

## Professional tax

A small state tax on employment, deducted monthly. Telangana levies it. You can see it on your payslip; it's also deductible from salary income in tax calculations.

:::trap
Comparing offers on CTC. Recruiters can inflate CTC with gratuity, insurance, one-time bonuses and optimistic variable pay. Always ask: **"What is the fixed monthly gross, and what's the expected in-hand?"**
:::
