:::hook
On Budget day, TV channels run for twelve hours. For most salaried people only a few lines matter: **income tax, taxes on investments, and a few savings schemes.** The rest still shapes the economy you work in, through interest rates, jobs and prices.
:::

## The government's household budget

**Money in**

- **Direct taxes**: personal income tax, corporate tax
- **Indirect taxes**: GST (shared with states), customs, excise on fuel
- **Non-tax income**: dividends from public sector companies and RBI, fees, sale of assets (disinvestment)

**Money out**

- **Revenue spending**: salaries, pensions, subsidies (food, fertiliser), **interest on past debt**
- **Capital spending (capex)**: roads, railways, defence equipment, power, things that last
- **Transfers to states**

## The fiscal deficit

**Fiscal deficit = total spending − total income (excluding borrowing)**

It's usually shown as a **% of GDP**. The government covers it by **borrowing**, mostly by selling government bonds (G-secs) and treasury bills to banks, insurers, mutual funds, provident funds and individuals.

:::example
Imagine a family earning ₹60,000 a month and spending ₹70,000. It borrows ₹10,000 a month. If it borrows to **build an extra room it rents out**, that can pay for itself. If it borrows **every month to pay for groceries**, the interest keeps growing and squeezes future budgets.

Governments work the same way. Interest on past borrowing is already one of the Union government's biggest expenses. The 2026-27 Budget set the fiscal deficit target at **4.3% of GDP**.
:::

## Why it matters to you

- **Interest rates**: heavy government borrowing competes with businesses and households for savings, which can keep rates higher. RBI watches the deficit when deciding the repo rate.
- **Inflation**: large spending financed by borrowing can add to price pressure.
- **Taxes**: income-tax slabs, deductions, capital gains and TDS rules change through the Finance Act. Since **1 April 2026**, income tax runs under the new **Income-tax Act, 2025** (Module 12).
- **Your future salary**: capex on roads, power and digital infrastructure can lift growth and hiring over years.

:::checklive incometax
After any Budget, check the Income Tax Department's site for what actually changed. Rules apply only once passed and notified.
:::

:::trap
Budget-day WhatsApp forwards and finfluencer "breaking" videos are often wrong or half-right. Wait for the official Budget speech, the Finance Bill, and later notifications. Proposals can change before they become law.
:::

:::dive States' budgets, and why GST is shared
India is a federation. States run most of health, education, police, agriculture and local roads, and present their own budgets, with their own deficits and borrowing (State Development Loans). GST is collected jointly and split between the Centre and states. The Finance Commission decides how the Centre's tax pool is shared among states. So Telangana's budget matters for Hyderabad's metro, hospitals and schools as much as the Union Budget does.
:::

:::dive You can lend to the government directly
Through **RBI Retail Direct**, individuals can buy government bonds and treasury bills directly, without a broker. These are the safest rupee investments (backed by the government), and their rates move with RBI policy and government borrowing. Module 8 covers when they make sense.
:::
