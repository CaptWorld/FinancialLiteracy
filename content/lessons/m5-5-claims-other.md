:::hook
Insurance only matters on the day you claim. Families lose money not because cover didn't exist, but because **nobody knew about the policy, the paperwork was missing, or a rejection went unchallenged.**
:::

## Making a claim go smoothly

**Health**
1. **Cashless**: at a network hospital, go to the insurance (TPA) desk with your e-card and ID. Planned treatment: get pre-authorisation in advance.
2. **Reimbursement**: inform the insurer within the time in your policy; keep **original bills, prescriptions, reports and the discharge summary**.
3. Check the final settlement letter for deductions and ask about any you don't understand.

**Life (for your nominee)**: death certificate, policy details, nominee's ID and bank details, and medical records if asked. Insurers are expected to process death claims quickly.

IRDAI's rules say a claim that's otherwise payable shouldn't be rejected just for late intimation or minor paperwork gaps. Insist on written reasons for any rejection.

## Motor insurance in one table

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Third-party (TP)** | Pays for injury/damage you cause to others. **Compulsory by law.** |
| **Own damage (OD)** | Pays to repair your own vehicle |
| **Comprehensive** | TP + OD together |
| **IDV** | Insured Declared Value, roughly market value. Max payout if stolen or totalled. |
| **NCB (No-Claim Bonus)** | Discount on OD premium for claim-free years; usually resets after a claim |
| **Zero depreciation add-on** | Pays full cost of replaced parts without depreciation; useful for newer vehicles |
| **Owner-driver personal accident** | Required with motor policies unless you already hold similar accident cover |

:::example
Arjun's bike: a ₹2,800 scrape. His NCB saves ₹1,500/year and grows for each claim-free year. If he claims, he loses the NCB for the next renewal, and rebuilding it takes years. **He pays himself.** For a ₹40,000 accident repair, he claims.
:::

## Personal accident and disability cover

Pays a lump sum if an accident causes death or disability, and some plans pay weekly income while you can't work. It's cheap and especially useful for two-wheeler commuters. Check whether your employer's group cover already includes it.

## When a claim is rejected: the free ladder

1. **Insurer's grievance officer**: write with your policy wording and documents. Ask for the reason in writing.
2. **IRDAI's Bima Bharosa system**: if the insurer doesn't resolve it, lodge it through IRDAI's official policyholder site. Use only the official site; IRDAI has warned about fake look-alikes.
3. **Insurance Ombudsman**: free. Approach after the insurer rejects your complaint or doesn't reply within **one month**, and within **one year**. Compensation sought must be within ₹50 lakh.
4. **Consumer court**: for larger or more complex disputes.

:::checklive cioins
The Ombudsman's site: how to file, office list, conditions.
:::

:::trap
Fake "insurance ombudsman" or "IRDAI" callers promise bonuses or refunds on old policies if you pay a "processing fee" or share details. **The Ombudsman never charges fees, never sells policies, and never calls to offer money.**
:::

:::dive The "family file": the 30-minute job that saves months
Make one document (paper or a shared note) for your family listing every policy (type, insurer, cover, nominee, where the document is), every bank and investment account (bank name and type only), and who to call. Tell your nominee it exists. Families often lose claims simply because they didn't know a policy existed. Module 16 covers wills and nominations properly.
:::
