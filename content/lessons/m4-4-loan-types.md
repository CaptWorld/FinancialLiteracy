Two people borrow ₹1 lakh for three months. One pays about ₹2,000 in interest. The other, with a loan app and late fees, ends up paying **₹40,000 and harassment calls to their contacts**. Same amount, same need. **Which loan you pick is the decision.**

## The map: cheapest to costliest

| Loan | Secured by | Cost (broadly) | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Overdraft / loan against FD** | Your FD | FD rate + ~1–2% | Ties up the FD until repaid |
| **Home loan** | The house | Lowest among big loans | 20–30 year commitment; tenure creep at resets |
| **Loan against mutual funds/shares** | Your investments | Low–moderate | Market fall → margin call or forced sale |
| **Gold loan** | Your gold | Low–moderate | Auction if unpaid; check the lender is RBI-regulated |
| **Car / two-wheeler loan** | The vehicle | Moderate | Vehicle loses value faster than the loan shrinks; dealer "flat rates" |
| **Education loan** | Sometimes collateral or co-borrower | Moderate | Interest runs during the course; parents often co-sign |
| **Personal loan** | Nothing | High | Fees, bundled insurance, long tenures |
| **Credit card EMI** | Nothing | High | "Conversion fees" + GST; still a loan |
| **Card revolving / BNPL late fees** | Nothing | Very high | Compounding, from purchase date |
| **Unregulated loan apps** | Your phone contacts 😟 | Extreme | Harassment, data misuse, hidden deductions |

Rates change with RBI's repo rate and your credit score. Get current numbers from KFSs, not from this table.

## Good debt vs bad debt

Ask three questions:

1. **Does it hold value or raise my income?** A home or a needed skill can. Phones, holidays and weddings don't.
2. **Is the rate reasonable?** Under your expected investment returns is good; personal-loan territory is not.
3. **Could I keep paying if my income stopped for 3–6 months?** If not, it's too big.

:::example
Arjun's take-home: ₹65,000. Bike EMI ₹3,877. If he adds a ₹3 lakh personal loan (₹10,108 EMI for 3 years):

- EMIs = ₹13,985 = **21.5%** of take-home
- Add rent ₹16,000 and ₹8,000 home → **58.4% committed** before food or travel

It can work, but one job gap and he's borrowing again. Smaller wedding budget? Part-saving for 6 months first? Those beat a bigger loan.
:::

## How much is too much?

Common guidelines from lenders and planners:

- **All EMIs together** below about **30–40% of take-home pay**. Lower is safer, especially if family depends on you.
- **Unsecured debt** (personal loans, card EMIs) kept small and short.
- **An emergency fund before borrowing**, so a surprise doesn't become another loan.

These are rules of thumb, not laws. Your own fixed costs matter more than any ratio.

:::trap
**Never borrow to invest**, whether in shares, F&O, crypto or a "scheme with fixed monthly returns". If the investment falls, you've lost the money and still owe the EMI. SEBI's own studies show most retail F&O traders lose money (Module 11).
:::

:::dive Education loans and home loans: what's special
- **Education loans**: interest usually builds up during the course and a short "moratorium" after. Repayment starts once you're earning. Tax deductions on interest exist for some regimes; check Module 12 or the Income Tax Department's site for the current rules. Co-borrowers (often parents) are equally liable, and it shows on their credit report.
- **Home loans**: banks lend up to a percentage of the property's value (the "loan-to-value" limit set by RBI), so you need a down payment. Also budget for stamp duty, registration and interiors, which usually aren't financed. Module 13 covers rent vs buy.
:::

:::dive How to check a lender is legitimate
- Banks and NBFCs are listed on RBI's website. Check the **lender's name**, not just the app's.
- RBI also maintains a directory of digital lending apps that regulated lenders have reported to it.
- Red flags: no KFS, asks for contacts/photos access, money arrives with a big deduction, pressure to decide in minutes, loan "approved" without checking anything.
- Report illegal apps at sachet.rbi.org.in and cybercrime.gov.in, or call 1930.
:::

:::checklive sachet
Check whether a lender or deposit scheme is authorised, or report one that isn't.
:::
