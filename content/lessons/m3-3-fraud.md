:::hook
Fraudsters don't hack banks. **They hack people**, using fear ("your account will be blocked"), authority ("this is CBI"), or greed ("earn ₹5,000 a day"). Learn the scripts and they stop working on you.
:::

## The scams you'll actually see

| Scam | Script | The tell |
|---|---|---|
| KYC/account block | "Your KYC expires today, share OTP" | Banks never ask for OTP |
| Fake customer care | You Google a helpline; the top result is a fraudster | Use numbers only from the official app/website |
| Screen-share | "Install AnyDesk so I can help" | Never let strangers see your screen |
| UPI collect / QR | "Scan to receive payment" | PIN only sends money |
| Digital arrest | Video call from "police/CBI/customs", parcel with drugs in your name | Agencies don't arrest or settle over video calls |
| Task / investment groups | Paid to like videos, then "invest for bigger tasks" | Guaranteed daily returns = scam |
| Fake loan apps | Instant loan, then harassment and huge charges | Check the lender is RBI-regulated (Module 4) |
| SIM swap | Phone suddenly loses signal; OTPs go to the fraudster | Call your telecom provider immediately |
| Job/recruitment fees | "Pay ₹5,000 for training/visa" | Real employers don't charge to hire |
| Fake "official" channels | A YouTube/Telegram/WhatsApp account named "SEBI", "RBI" or a famous investor offers tips or a group | Regulators don't run tip groups. Reach their social accounts only from links on their official website |

:::checklive rbi-cms
RBI's complaint site links its BE(A)WARE booklet on fraud methods. Read it once; it's short and from the regulator.
:::

## Five habits that stop most fraud

1. **Never share** OTP, PIN, CVV, passwords. Nobody legitimate asks.
2. **Hang up and call back** on a number from the official app or card, not one they give you.
3. **No screen-sharing** apps for strangers.
4. **Pause when rushed.** Real problems can wait 10 minutes for you to check.
5. **Alerts on every account** so you see debits instantly.

## If money is stolen: the first hour

:::task
Save this now so you don't have to think during panic:
1. **Call 1930** and report on **cybercrime.gov.in**. Note the acknowledgement number.
2. **Call your bank** on the official number to block cards, UPI and net banking.
3. **Write down** every transaction ID, time, amount, and the fraudster's number/UPI ID/website.
4. **Report in writing** to your bank (email/app) so there's a timestamp.
5. **Don't pay "recovery agents"**. That's a second scam.
:::

:::checklive cybercrime
Bookmark the portal. Note that the helpline number is shown on the home page.
:::

:::checklive rbi-faqs
Find RBI's FAQ on customer liability in unauthorised electronic transactions. The faster you report, the lower your liability can be. Note how many working days count as "quick".
:::

## Protect your family too

- Check **Sanchar Saathi** for SIMs issued in your and your parents' names.
- Report fraud calls/SMS through its **Chakshu** feature.
- Use **Sachet** to check if a "scheme" someone's pitching is collecting deposits illegally.

:::checklive sancharsaathi
Check how many mobile connections are registered in your name. Report any you don't recognise.
:::

:::checklive sachet
Look at how to report an entity collecting deposits or promising unrealistic returns.
:::
