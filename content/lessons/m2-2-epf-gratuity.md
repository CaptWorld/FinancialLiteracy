:::hook
Every month a chunk of your salary vanishes into "PF". Most people only think about it when they switch jobs and wonder whether to withdraw it. **It's probably your biggest investment right now. Treat it like one.**
:::

## How EPF works

- You contribute a set percentage of your **wages** (basic + DA, with the Labour Codes' 50% rule) every month.
- Your employer contributes too. Part goes to your **EPF** account and part to **EPS** (the Employees' Pension Scheme).
- EPFO declares an **interest rate each year**; it's credited on your monthly running balance.
- You can contribute extra voluntarily (VPF) through your employer.

:::checklive epfo
Find the latest declared EPF interest rate (look under press releases / circulars). Note the financial year it applies to. Don't trust a number from a forwarded message.
:::

## UAN: one number, whole career

Your **UAN** stays the same across jobs. Each new employer links a new member ID to it. Things to do once:

1. Activate your UAN on the member portal.
2. Make sure KYC (bank, PAN, Aadhaar) is linked. Don't share these numbers in this app or with anyone who calls you.
3. Add an **e-nomination**.
4. When you change jobs, **transfer** the old balance into your UAN.

:::checklive epfo-member
Log in, check KYC status and nominee. If no nominee is listed, add one today.
:::

## Check that your money is actually there

Your payslip shows PF *deducted*. Only the **passbook** shows it *deposited*.

:::checklive epfo-passbook
Open the passbook for your current member ID. Check the last 3 months have employee and employer credits. If they're missing, ask HR in writing, then use EPFiGMS.
:::

## Withdrawals: the October 2025 reform

In October 2025 EPFO's Central Board of Trustees simplified partial withdrawals. Officially:

- **13 separate withdrawal reasons were merged into three**: essential needs (illness, education, marriage), housing needs, and special circumstances.
- A **minimum balance of 25%** of contributions plus interest must stay in the account.
- On unemployment, up to **75% can be withdrawn immediately**, the rest later (the government release says after one year).
- Minimum service for most withdrawals was standardised at one year.

:::checklive pib-epf-withdrawal
Read the official release. Rules and waiting periods have been adjusted more than once. If you're actually planning a withdrawal, re-check the current EPFO FAQ before applying.
:::

:::trap
Withdrawing PF on every job switch. You lose decades of compounding, may owe tax if your continuous service is short, and break your pension service history. Transfer instead.
:::

## Gratuity

A lump sum your employer pays when you leave after qualifying service, as thanks for long service.

- **Formula (roughly)**: 15 days' wages × completed years of service, based on your **last drawn wages**. Service over 6 months in the final year usually counts as a full year.
- **Eligibility**: typically 5 years of continuous service (exceptions for death or disability). **Fixed-term employees** qualify after **1 year** under the Labour Codes.
- **Cap**: there's a maximum set by the government. Gratuity above the tax-free limit may be taxed.
- Since **21 Nov 2025**, gratuity uses the new "wages" definition (the 50% rule), which can raise it.

:::checklive labour-faq
Read the FAQ section on gratuity: how it's calculated and when it's payable. Note the current maximum if listed, or where it's notified.
:::

:::example
Illustration: last drawn wages ₹50,000/month, 6 years 8 months of service.
Years counted: 7 (since 8 months > 6 months).
Gratuity ≈ 50,000 × 15/26 × 7 ≈ **₹2,01,923**. (The traditional formula divides monthly wages by 26 working days. Check your employer's policy and the current rules.)
:::
