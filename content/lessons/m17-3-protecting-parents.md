:::hook
"Digital arrest" scams, where fraudsters on video calls pose as police or CBI, have cost Indian families, especially older people, enormous sums. The victims weren't careless. They were frightened, isolated on a call for hours, and told not to tell anyone. **The defence is preparation before the call comes.**
:::

## The scams that target parents

| Scam | How it sounds | The tell |
|---|---|---|
| **Digital arrest** | "CBI/customs/police: your Aadhaar is linked to a crime. Stay on video, don't tell anyone." | No agency arrests or collects money on a call |
| **KYC / pension update** | "Your account/pension will stop; click this link or share OTP" | Banks and pension offices don't ask for OTPs |
| **Remote-access "support"** | "Install AnyDesk/TeamViewer to fix your account" | Never install apps for callers |
| **Fake investments / gold schemes** | "Guaranteed 2% monthly", "special senior scheme" | Check on Sachet; high return + safety = scam |
| **Relative in trouble** | "Your son is in an accident, send money now" | Hang up and call the relative directly |
| **Electricity bill / courier** | "Pay now or power is cut tonight" | Pay only via the official app or website |

## Safeguards that respect independence

Set these up **with** them, not **for** them:

- **Alerts**: SMS and app notifications for every debit.
- **Limits**: lower UPI and online-transaction limits on their accounts.
- **A family rule**: "Before any payment that's unusual or urgent, call one of us first. We'll never be upset."
- **Phone hygiene**: avoid unknown apps; block spam numbers; Sanchar Saathi helps report fraud calls and check mobile connections in their name.
- **Rehearse**: once, calmly, walk through what a digital-arrest call sounds like.

:::checklive cybercrime
The national cybercrime portal. The helpline 1930 takes reports of financial fraud; calling quickly improves the chance of freezing the money.
:::

## Health costs: the biggest financial risk

- **Health insurance**: senior plans exist with no maximum entry age; disclose conditions honestly (waiting periods and co-pay may apply). Check whether your employer's cover can include parents (Module 5).
- **Medical buffer**: a separate amount in a savings account, sweep-in FD or liquid fund for costs insurance won't cover.
- **Records**: a folder (or shared note) with prescriptions, reports, policy details and the insurer's cashless helpline.

## Continuity: money must stay accessible

- **Joint accounts** in "Either or Survivor" mode, so either can operate and the survivor continues.
- **Nominees** on every account and policy; **wills** for both parents (Module 16).
- A **family file** you and a sibling know about.
- Consider who could manage things if a parent became unwell; a lawyer can advise on options like a power of attorney.

:::trap
"Helping" by holding parents' cards, PINs or logins creates risk for everyone and confusion about responsibility. Better to set up alerts, limits, nominees and joint accounts properly.
:::
