title: How to verify a finance claim
reviewed_on: 2026-11-02

Most bad money decisions start with a claim nobody checked. Use these five questions every time.

## 1. What's the primary source?

Find the original: an RBI notification, a SEBI circular, an Income Tax Department page, a fund's factsheet, a law on India Code. If someone can't point to one, treat the claim as a rumour.

| Topic | Primary source |
|---|---|
| Banking, loans, cards, UPI rules | RBI notifications, RBI FAQs, NPCI |
| Mutual funds, stocks, advisers | SEBI circulars, AMFI, fund factsheets/SID |
| Insurance | IRDAI, the policy wording (not the brochure) |
| Tax | incometax.gov.in, the Income-tax Act 2025 and Rules 2026 |
| EPF, gratuity, labour rules | EPFO, Ministry of Labour, PIB releases |
| Inflation | MoSPI CPI |

## 2. Who benefits?

Does the person earn from your action: commission, affiliate links, course sales, broker referrals? That doesn't make them wrong, but it means you must check harder. Fee-only SEBI-registered investment advisers are paid by you, not by products.

## 3. When was it true?

Tax slabs, interest rates, limits and rules change, often every year. A 2023 article about tax is probably out of date. Check the date of the source and whether it has been superseded. In this app, every lesson shows a "reviewed on" date and warns you after 12 months.

## 4. What's the evidence against it?

Search for the opposite claim. For investing, look for long-term data (SEBI studies, SPIVA India for active vs index funds) rather than one good year or one person's story.

## 5. Does it apply to me?

Old vs new tax regime, salaried vs business income, age, state: many rules depend on these. A rule that's true for someone else may not apply to you.

## Red flags in finance content

- Guaranteed high returns, or "no risk"
- Urgency: "only today", "limited seats", "before the rule changes"
- Screenshots of profits instead of verifiable track records
- Telegram/WhatsApp "tips" groups, "signals", copy trading
- Courses that promise you can replace your salary through trading
- No mention of costs, taxes or what can go wrong
- The person isn't SEBI-registered but gives specific buy/sell advice

## Reading official documents without fear

- **SEBI circular / RBI notification**: read the subject line, the "applicability" and "effective date" paragraphs first. The rest is detail.
- **Budget**: the Budget speech is a summary of proposals; what becomes law is the Finance Act. Check the Income Tax Department's pages after the Act passes.
- **Loan agreement**: look for the rate type (fixed/floating, flat/reducing), all fees, prepayment charges, and what happens if you miss a payment. Ask for the Key Fact Statement.
